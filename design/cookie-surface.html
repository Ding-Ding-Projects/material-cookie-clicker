Cookie surface · 曲奇表面 — primary click target

The main <button> the whole game is built on. Minimum 88×88px hit target (shown here at 96px), rendered with layered CSS radial-gradients for the chocolate-chip texture — no image asset. States below: rest, hover, pressed, focus-visible, and reduced-motion. The floating "+N" popup and the golden-cookie overlay are shown as separate labelled variants since they compose onto the base button rather than replacing it. 成個遊戲嘅主按鈕。最少 88×88px 嘅可點擊範圍，用純CSS漸變畫出朱古力粒質感，唔使用圖片。

Interaction states · 互動狀態

Rest · 靜止

Default idle state. Radial gradient gives it dimensionality without a bitmap.

Hover · 滑鼠移入

Scales to 1.04x. Disabled entirely under reduced-motion (see below).

Pressed · 撳落

Scales to 0.94x on :active. Same reduced-motion exemption applies.

Focus-visible · 鍵盤焦點

Keyboard-reachable; tertiary-coloured ring meets 3:1 non-text contrast against the surface.

Reduced motion · 減少動態

When prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, hover/press scale transforms are disabled entirely (this cell has .reduced-motion applied to demonstrate — try hovering it, nothing moves). A brief opacity pulse replaces the scale in the shipped app instead of removing feedback altogether.

Disabled · 停用

Used during the brief input-lock after a bulk offline-earnings modal closes.

Floating "+N" popup · 浮動 "+N" 彈出

Spawns above the click point on every click, drifts upward, fades out over ~700ms (or a static 400ms hold under reduced-motion). This is not a notification — it never persists, never stacks in a history, and is purely decorative feedback for the click that just happened.

Golden-cookie click · 金曲奇一擊

Uses the tertiary role exclusively — the one place in the app tertiary appears as a solid fill.

Golden-cookie overlay · 金曲奇覆蓋層

A time-limited bonus spawn: a soft tertiary-container glow plus a solid tertiary ring around the same 96px button, replacing its texture with a brighter variant while it is live (typically 13–15s). Clicking it triggers the golden-cookie popup above and a matching non-blocking toast (see narrator-toast.html).
