Cookie surface · 曲奇表面 — primary click target

The main <button> the whole game is built on. Minimum 88×88px hit target (shown here at 104px), rendered with layered CSS radial-gradients for the chocolate-chip texture — no image asset. Depth is a solid, offset "arcade cabinet" shadow in --md-primary-shadow that compresses flat when pressed, not a Material 3 blur. States below: rest, hover, pressed, focus-visible, and reduced-motion. The floating "+N" popup and the golden-cookie overlay are shown as separate labelled variants since they compose onto the base button rather than replacing it. 成個遊戲嘅主按鈕。最少 88×88px 嘅可點擊範圍，用純CSS漸變畫出朱古力粒質感，唔使用圖片。撳落去嗰陣個影會壓扁，好似街機掣咁。

Interaction states · 互動狀態

Rest · 靜止

Default idle state. The offset shadow beneath it is what makes it read as "press me", before any hover or click.

Hover · 滑鼠移入

Lifts slightly and the shadow grows, as if it's rising off the counter. Disabled entirely under reduced-motion (see below).

Pressed · 撳落

Drops down by the shadow's own offset and the shadow disappears — the cookie visibly compresses into the counter. Same reduced-motion exemption applies.

Focus-visible · 鍵盤焦點

Keyboard-reachable; tertiary-coloured ring meets 3:1 non-text contrast against the surface.

Reduced motion · 減少動態

When prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, hover/press transforms are disabled entirely (this cell has .reduced-motion applied to demonstrate — try hovering or clicking it, nothing moves). The box-shadow still snaps instantly from full depth to flat on press — an un-animated state change, not motion — so the "pressed" feedback survives without any movement.

Disabled · 停用

Used during the brief input-lock after a bulk offline-earnings modal closes.

Floating "+N" popup · 浮動 "+N" 彈出

Spawns above the click point on every click, drifts upward, fades out over ~700ms (or a static 400ms hold under reduced-motion). This is not a notification — it never persists, never stacks in a history, and is purely decorative feedback for the click that just happened. Set in the display face with tabular numerals so rapid clicking never jitters the digits.

Golden-cookie click · 金曲奇一擊

Uses the tertiary role exclusively — the one place in the app tertiary appears as a solid fill.

Golden-cookie overlay · 金曲奇覆蓋層

A time-limited bonus spawn: a bright --spark glow, a chunky --spark-ring border, and a slow-spinning ray burst around the same 104px button, replacing its texture with a brighter variant while it is live (typically 13–15s). Clicking it triggers the golden-cookie popup above and a matching non-blocking toast (see narrator-toast.html). Under reduced motion the pulse and spin stop but the glow, ring, and rays stay fully visible and static — the "something special is here" signal never depends on the motion.
