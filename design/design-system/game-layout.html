Game layout · 遊戲版面

The single main game surface. Material Cookie Clicker's moment-to-moment loop — click the cookie, buy a generator, buy an upgrade — happens on one screen with no navigation between those three things. The cookie is the hero, the generator shop is docked to it as a right rail, and upgrades are a compact ticket strip on the same surface. Buying never takes the player away from the cookie, and the cookie is never hidden behind a tab. 遊戲主畫面只有一個：撳曲奇、買生產器、買升級全部喺同一版，唔使切換頁面。曲奇係主角，商店貼喺右邊，升級係下面一條票仔橫條。

The rule · 規則 Shops, upgrades and the cookie are never on different pages. Tabs exist only for genuinely secondary surfaces a player visits deliberately and briefly — statistics, prestige, the tools tech tree, the achievements gallery. If a control participates in the core loop, it lives on this surface. 商店同升級永遠唔會分開放喺唔同版面；只有統計、轉生、工具科技樹、成就呢啲次要畫面先用分頁。

Wide layout · 闊版面

Three regions on one surface: the pinned HUD readouts across the top, the hero column (cookie plus the upgrade ticket strip directly beneath it, within thumb reach of the cookie), and the shop rail docked to the right. The rail scrolls internally; the cookie never scrolls away.

Narrow layout · 窄版面

Below roughly 860px the rail cannot sit beside the cookie, so it becomes a bottom-sheet drawer on the same surface — not a separate screen. The HUD stays pinned at the top, the cookie stays visible above the sheet, and the sheet is dragged or tapped down rather than navigated away from. The upgrade strip stays a horizontally-scrolling strip; it is never moved into the drawer, because upgrades and generators are two different decisions.

Rules this layout encodes · 呢個版面定死嘅規矩

  • No navigation inside the loop. Clicking, buying a generator and buying an upgrade are reachable without a single route change. 核心循環唔准換頁。
  • The cookie is never occluded by a purchase surface. The drawer covers at most the lower portion of the narrow layout and the cookie stays visible and clickable above it.
  • The HUD readouts are pinned and use the same recessed bezel as stat-tile.html, so a number means the same thing everywhere.
  • Tabs are for secondary surfaces only — statistics, achievements, the tools tech tree, prestige, settings. Adding a core-loop control to a tab is a spec violation.
  • Every control here is ≥44px, keyboard reachable in reading order, and focus-ringed in the spark accent; the drawer is a region on the same document, not a modal, so focus is never trapped.
  • Reduced motion: the press-compression on the cookie, the tickets and the shop rows becomes an instant inset state change rather than a transition, and the drawer snaps between positions instead of sliding.
