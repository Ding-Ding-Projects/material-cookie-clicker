Game layout · 遊戲版面

The single main game surface. Material Cookie Clicker's moment-to-moment loop — click the cookie, buy a generator, buy an upgrade — happens on one screen with no navigation between those three things. The cookie is the hero, the generator shop is docked to it as a right rail, and upgrades are a compact ticket strip on the same surface. Buying never takes the player away from the cookie, and the cookie is never hidden behind a tab. 遊戲主畫面只有一個：撳曲奇、買生產器、買升級全部喺同一版，唔使切換頁面。曲奇係主角，商店貼喺右邊，升級係下面一條票仔橫條。

The rule · 規則 Shops, upgrades and the cookie are never on different pages — and neither are the secondary surfaces. There is no tab bar at all: statistics, prestige, the tools tech tree and the achievements gallery each open as an anchored dialog on top of the game, which stays visible and keeps ticking behind them. If a control participates in the core loop, it lives on this surface. 商店同升級永遠唔會分開放喺唔同版面；統計、轉生、工具科技樹、成就係喺遊戲畫面之上彈出嘅面板，唔係分頁。

Wide layout · 闊版面

Three regions on one surface: the pinned HUD readouts across the top, the hero column (cookie plus the upgrade ticket strip directly beneath it, within thumb reach of the cookie), and the shop rail docked to the right. The rail scrolls internally; the cookie never scrolls away.

Anchored dialogs · 定點彈窗

Pressing a console button does not change page. The surface grows out of that button as a modal dialog over the game: the cap stays pressed in, the panel's transform-origin is the cap's centre, and a notch on the panel's top edge points back at it. The game is dimmed but still there, and the tick loop keeps running behind the scrim.

Prestige 轉生

The surface's own content goes here and scrolls inside the panel — the page body never scrolls. The 44px close button is always present, so the backdrop is never the only way out. 面板內部自己滾動。

Narrow layout · 窄版面

Below roughly 860px the rail cannot sit beside the cookie, so it becomes a bottom-sheet drawer on the same surface — not a separate screen. The HUD stays pinned at the top, the cookie stays visible above the sheet, and the sheet is dragged or tapped down rather than navigated away from. The upgrade strip stays a horizontally-scrolling strip; it is never moved into the drawer, because upgrades and generators are two different decisions.

Rules this layout encodes · 呢個版面定死嘅規矩

  • No navigation inside the loop. Clicking, buying a generator and buying an upgrade are reachable without a single route change. 核心循環唔准換頁。
  • The cookie is never occluded by a purchase surface. The drawer covers at most the lower portion of the narrow layout and the cookie stays visible and clickable above it.
  • The HUD readouts are pinned and use the same recessed bezel as stat-tile.html, so a number means the same thing everywhere.
  • No tab bar exists. The four secondary surfaces are reached from the console cluster and open as anchored dialogs over the game; the game surface is always the base and is never replaced. Adding a core-loop control to one of those panels is still a spec violation. 唔好再用分頁列。
  • The anchored dialog is a real dialog. role="dialog", aria-modal="true", labelled by its own header, focus moved into it on open and trapped until it closes, Escape closes, a click on the dimmed game behind closes, and focus returns to the button that opened it. The 44px close button is always present — the backdrop is never the only way out. The panel scrolls internally; the body never scrolls.
  • It grows from its button. transform-origin is set to the pressed button's centre and the notch points back at it, so the panel reads as that button opening rather than as a web modal dropped over the page.
  • Console buttons are buttons, not tabs. aria-haspopup="dialog" plus aria-expanded, each with a bilingual accessible name, all ≥44px, reachable in reading order — no roving tabindex, because nothing here is a tablist.
  • Every control here is ≥44px, keyboard reachable in reading order, and focus-ringed in the spark accent; the drawer is a region on the same document, not a modal, so focus is never trapped.
  • Reduced motion: the press-compression on the cookie, the tickets and the shop rows becomes an instant inset state change rather than a transition, the drawer snaps between positions instead of sliding, the console's sheen and emblem float stop entirely, and the anchored panel appears and disappears with no scale, slide or fade.
