Milestone toast · 里程碑提示

A non-blocking, corner-anchored notification for game milestones (achievement unlocked, golden cookie missed, offline earnings collected). Always bottom-right in this spec; the app's own notification settings may move it to bottom-left. Two lifecycle variants: an auto-dismissing informational toast, and a persist-until-dismissed variant for anything the player should actively acknowledge (e.g. a large offline-earnings summary). In v2 it is built as a cabinet marquee: a tinted, letter-spaced all-caps header plate that names the kind of moment in words (not colour alone), over a recessed message face, on the same fat border and 6px solid offset base as every other plate in the theme. The golden variant's marquee bulbs blink; under prefers-reduced-motion they hold steady at full brightness and the tint plus the wording still carry the meaning. 非阻擋式、貼喺角落嘅提示，達成里程碑先出現。分自動消失同要用戶主動關閉兩種。

Auto-dismiss variant · 自動消失版本

Fires on a golden-cookie click; fades out on its own after ~5s. Hovering or focusing pauses the timer. The spark-accent left border marks it as a golden-cookie moment specifically.

Persist-until-dismissed variant · 需手動關閉版本

Used for the offline-earnings summary shown on app launch. Stays until the player dismisses it or opens the full breakdown; never blocks input to the rest of the app.
