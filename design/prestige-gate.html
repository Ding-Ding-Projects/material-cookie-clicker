Prestige / wipe gate · 轉生／清空閘門 — super-confirmation

The destructive-action gate. Two independently operated key toggles, both required before the confirmation slider unlocks; a dramatic non-blocking progress fill while dragging; a distinct completion state; and an always-available Emergency exit. Rendered chunkier than a settings dialog on purpose — this is the one moment the game wants to feel weighty and deliberate rather than quick. This page's gates are live and interactive — drag the sliders yourself once both keys are on to see the disabled state actually enforced. 危險動作嘅雙重確認閘門：兩條獨立嘅開關掣要一齊開先可以郁到確認滑桿，仲有隨時可以撳嘅緊急退出掣。

Variant 1 — Prestige (recoverable) · 轉生（可以再賺返）

Copy names exactly what resets and what carries forward. Prestige is a normal, expected, repeatable action — not "as severe" as a wipe — so it uses the tertiary/primary palette rather than error.

Variant 2 — Full wipe (severe) · 完全清空（嚴重）

Nothing carries forward from a full wipe, so the copy says exactly that and the gate uses the error role throughout rather than tertiary/primary.
