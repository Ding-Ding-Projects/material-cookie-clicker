Material Cookie Clicker design tokens · 設計標記 — Colour

Full Material Design 3 colour role set for Material Cookie Clicker, themed as a warm baked-cookie brown/gold primary with the golden-cookie accent reserved for tertiary. Every role pair used for text has a real, computed WCAG contrast ratio shown on the swatch — not asserted, calculated with the sRGB relative-luminance formula in design/_verify/contrast-check.mjs and re-verified before this file shipped.
