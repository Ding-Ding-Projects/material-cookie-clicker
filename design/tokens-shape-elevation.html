Material Cookie Clicker design tokens · 設計標記 — Shape & Elevation

Corner radii and elevation levels, each rendered on a real card so the shadow can be judged directly rather than read as a number. 形狀圓角同明度層級，每一個都畫喺真卡片上面，等你親眼睇到個陰影，唔使靠估。

Shape scale · 形狀階

Used across the app: full for the cookie surface and pill buttons, large for dialogs and the prestige gate, medium for building rows and upgrade cards, small/extra-small for chips and badges.

Elevation levels · 明度層級

M3 uses tonal elevation (a lighter/darker surface tint) plus a shadow; this spec shows the shadow component so the depth is judgeable against the page background at each level. Level 0 is flush with the page; level 5 is reserved for the prestige-gate dialog, the highest-priority surface in the app.

--elev-0 (flush, e.g. building-row at rest)

--elev-1 (cards, stat tiles)
