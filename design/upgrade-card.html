Upgrade card · 升級卡

A one-time purchase card (doubles a building's CPS, unlocks a click multiplier, etc). Three states: locked (requirement not yet met), unlocked-and-buyable, and owned (permanent, no re-purchase, struck in the golden-cookie tone). In v2 the card is shaped as a ticket: notches punched out of both long edges, a dashed perforation above the price stub, and the price itself set as a big all-caps tabular stub button on its own 6px base. A live ticket tilts a couple of degrees on hover — pure delight, and disabled entirely under reduced motion, where the ticket still reads as buyable from its shape, fill and stub. 單次購買嘅升級卡，分三種狀態：未解鎖、可以買，同埋已經擁有。
