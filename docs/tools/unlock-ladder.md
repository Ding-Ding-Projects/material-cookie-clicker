# Unlock ladder

## Behavior

After authentication lockout, the ladder offers dim-sum identification, ten easy sums,
whack-a-mole, then the original clock. It clears waiting only, never credentials or attempt budget.

## Configuration

At most three waits may be skipped per rolling hour. Challenges use expiring, server-graded,
single-use nonces; School mode starts at sums.

## Failure modes

Neither baseline surface authenticates through this ladder. No implementation, tests, built
interaction, or capture exists.

## Security and privacy

Winning never signs in or creates a session. Lockout escalation remains unchanged, timed games
cannot finish early, and each mole is graded once.

## Verification

Required coverage includes every rung/failure, budget refill, replay/expiry, early submissions, and
proof that no session cookie is created.
