Achievements

Two hundred and one achievements, generated from eleven milestone families, each one pouring another 4% of milk.

Current state

Shipped the Achievements tab is in this release; 201 definitions in src/shared/game/achievements.ts.

From the running application

See all nine captures in the capture matrix.

What it does

An achievement is a named milestone that unlocks permanently the first time its condition is met, and records the moment it was unlocked. There are 201 definitions, built from eleven families:

  • 120 ownership milestones — 1, 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 owned, for each of the twenty generators.
  • 15 lifetime-cookie milestones — your first cookie, then thresholds up to 1036, plus the named First Bite.
  • 9 click milestones — from 100 clicks up to ten million.
  • 7 prestige milestones — ascension counts from 1 up to 250.
  • 9 total-machine milestones — every machine on every rung added together, from 10 up to 10,000.
  • 6 ladder-breadth milestones — distinct rungs occupied, up to all twenty.
  • 9 upgrades-bought milestones — from your first upgrade up to 150.
  • 10 milk-ladder milestones — badges for collecting badges, one per milk flavour band. Each one also pours another 4% of milk, which is exactly as circular as it sounds.
  • 5 Reborn milestones — nodes bought in the permanent tree.
  • 11 Diesel Depot milestones — litres minted and vouchers printed, counted separately, because a hundred one-litre mints and one hundred-litre mint are not the same story about a player.

Achievements are also the milk supply: each unlocked badge pours 4% more milk into the cabinet, which the kitten upgrade line converts into production. Every achievement has both an English and a Cantonese name. Conditions are evaluated against game state, which means a milestone crossed by passive production counts exactly like one crossed by clicking — achievements are not checked only at purchase time.

How it is configured

The milestone thresholds are arrays at the top of one file, and the definitions are generated from them, so the count moves with the data instead of being hand-maintained. design/achievement-badge.html fixes the presentation: a locked silhouette, an unlocked struck medal with a domed face and a rotating conic metal rim, and a distinct unlock toast that is corner-anchored, auto-dismissing, and never a blocking dialog.

The properties that matter

Unlocks are permanent and survive prestige. An unlocked achievement never silently re-locks. Because there is no leaderboard, no server and no payment anywhere in this product, an edited save file granting every badge is not a security problem and is not treated as one.

In the shipped build

The Achievements tab shows all one hundred badges with a live unlocked count and a search field, locked ones as grey silhouettes. The unlock toast works — and can briefly overlay a shop row on the game surface, which is a known rough edge rather than a hidden one.
