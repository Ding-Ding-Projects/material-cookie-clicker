The 14-tier generator ladder

Cursor through Prism: fourteen generator tiers, a constant 1.15 cost curve, and the buy-quantity stepper.

Current state

Rules engine: built and unit-tested (src/shared/game/generators.ts, 14 definitions)

Player-facing screen: roadmap, not built

What it does

Fourteen generator tiers bake cookies automatically once owned. Each tier's base cost and base output are roughly ten times the previous tier's, so the “the next tier is finally worth it” moment recurs at a predictable cadence for the whole run. Every tier carries an English name and a Hong Kong Cantonese name, and the Cantonese names lean into local jokes rather than literal translation.

Every tier uses the same per-unit cost growth ratio of 1.15, held constant across the whole ladder rather than tuned per tier, so the price of the next unit is a single predictable formula the player can reason about.

How it is configured

Cost, output and growth ratio are data in one file, not scattered through the purchase logic. A row's three presentation states are fixed by design/building-row.html: affordable, unaffordable (visible but disabled, never hidden), and locked (shown-and-locked, so you always know what is coming). The buy-quantity stepper offers ×1, ×10, ×100 and Max as a segmented cabinet switch.

The rule about locks

Locking a later tier behind an earlier one's progress is a pacing decision for the game. It gates a purchase, and only a purchase. It is unrelated to the Tools tech tree, which never gates an application feature.

Roadmap, not shipped

There is no generator list on screen yet, and no buy stepper. The ladder data, the cost curve and the CPS contribution maths are what exists today.
