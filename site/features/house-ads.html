House ads

Non-blocking, dismissible cards that promote the project’s own real features — no third party, no network, no payment.

Current state

Implemented the site renders one bundled house-ad card after first paint, persists dismissals locally, and exposes global, per-card, bulk, export, and reset controls in the Control Center. Built-site interaction and capture evidence remain open.

Behavior

A house ad is a small, dismissible card that promotes one feature the game already ships — the diesel factory, the Tools tech tree, the changelog viewer, or any other real, in-app surface. It names the feature, links to its destination or its feature article, and carries a close control. Dismissing a card keeps it hidden across reloads until house ads are reset in the Control Center. Nothing about a house ad blocks input, steals focus, or interrupts whatever the player is doing — it follows the same non-blocking notification rules as every other toast in the interface.

Configuration

There is no third party anywhere in this feature. Every card is authored content bundled inside the repository, pointing only at a real in-app destination or a real feature article — never an external advertiser, a tracking pixel, or a paid placement. The rotation is a fixed, bundled list; nothing is fetched at runtime to decide what to show. The Control Center provides a global on/off switch, per-card enable/disable controls, bulk actions, export, and reset. Cards follow the same language modes and per-language funny-level sliders as the rest of the interface: the promoted feature and its destination stay factual at every level, and only the surrounding voice changes.

Failure modes

An empty or malformed bundled card list simply shows no card; a missing promotion is not an error a player needs to see. A card whose destination has since been removed or renamed is a documentation defect to fix in the card list, not a runtime failure to paper over with a broken link.

Security and privacy

No network request of any kind: no ad network, no CDN, no analytics, no tracking, and no impression count sent anywhere. Nothing here costs the player money — a house ad promotes a feature already included in the same free, unsigned build, and it is never a purchase prompt. Dismissal state lives in local application storage only.

Verification

tests/site-house-ads.test.ts checks the bundled catalogue, local-only behavior, linked targets, Material surface tokens, metadata, and documentation contract. scripts/check-site.mjs validates the complete static site. See the repository’s per-surface completeness inventory for the remaining built-site interaction and capture evidence gap.
