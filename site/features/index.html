Where the project actually is

The game is playable, and it starts plain on purpose — the whole arcade-bakery look is a seven-rung purchase, as are the window’s own buttons, the Settings panel and the search fields. Past that, this release ships the game surface (HUD readouts, the raid-supplies shelf, the cookie hero, the docked shop rail with the Diesel Depot status card in its footer, and the upgrade shelf) plus the Diesel Factory, The Home, Achievements, Tools, Statistics, Prestige, Prices and Settings panels, sixteen random events, the Mouse Raid, milk and the Reborn tree, the wandering golden cookie with its Oven Dial minigame, and the automatic-update notice. The later verification pass reported 903/903 local tests and a successful build. The capture matrix is an honest historical gallery rather than a claim that every current panel has a release-tip screenshot; it lists the remaining gaps.

Two things are still honestly unverified, and they stay listed here until someone has actually seen them: the narrow-width shop drawer was verified by forcing its breakpoint rather than by really resizing the window, since every capture is one maximised window; and no golden-cookie spawn has been photographed — the general random-event pool has been, but the golden cookie is a separate schedule and remains uncaught. The dark theme is no longer on this list — it was captured from the built application.
