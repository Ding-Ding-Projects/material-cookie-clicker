All features / Minigames

Minigame events and Lucky Drawer

At 100,000 lifetime baked cookies, a seeded side-panel schedule begins offering five minigames without pausing the clicker.

Unlocks and timing

The minigame panel unlocks permanently at 100,000 lifetime baked cookies. Mouse Raids unlock permanently at 1,000,000 lifetime baked cookies. Prestige keeps both thresholds because baked-cookie history is preserved. The prestige tab remains at 1B and the prestige action remains at 1T.

The schedule chooses a start 6–12 minutes after the previous opportunity. Its notice is hidden until the final 30 seconds. There is one active minigame at a time, and the cookie loop continues while its anchored panel is open.

Five persisted boards

  • Klondike Solitaire: draw-three stock, legal tableau/foundation moves, recycling, and face-up state.
  • Memory Match: shuffled pairs, safe reveal/match transitions, and attempts.
  • Cookie 2048: directional merges, seeded tile spawning, score, and win state.
  • Minesweeper: safe first reveal, flood reveal, flags, chords, and win/loss state.
  • Breakout: paddle, ball vector, bricks, score, lives, pause, and win state.

Minimize, resume, restart, and abandon are persisted lifecycle operations. A reload returns to the exact saved board rather than generating a replacement.

Golden Tokens and Lucky Chance

Golden Tokens come from Oven Dial completion, minigame grades, daily objectives, achievement milestones, and rare chains. The ledger is duplicate-protected and action-based, so offline progress cannot farm tokens.

The Lucky Chance drawer costs one Golden Token. It discloses five equal reward slots and can grant cookie bundles, timed boosts, raid supplies, or rare cosmetics. The seeded roll, injected timestamp, token deduction, result, and duplicate-protection state are persisted together.

Failure and recovery

Older saves without the minigame sidecar load with an empty suite. The sidecar carries the active board and reward ledgers separately from the versioned core save, so the clicker remains recoverable if no minigame progress exists.

This expedited release intentionally has no new test, capture, or UI-run evidence; that verification remains pending.
