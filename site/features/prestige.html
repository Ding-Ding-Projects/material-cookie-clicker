Prestige (ascend)

A cube-root ascension curve, +1% production per point, behind a two-key destructive-action gate.

Current state

Rules engine: built and unit-tested (src/shared/game/prestige.ts)

Player-facing screen: roadmap, not built

What it does

Ascending resets your cookies, your lifetime total and every owned generator, and converts your progress into permanent ascension points. Each point adds +1% to total production, and that formula lives in exactly one place, imported by the multiplier computation rather than restated.

Points earned are the genre-standard cube root of your lifetime cookies divided by a trillion, floored to a whole number. Ascending is only possible once lifetime cookies reach a trillion.

What survives an ascension is explicit: ascension points, total prestige count, permanent unlocks, achievements and statistics. What is reset: cookies, lifetime cookies, generator counts, and every upgrade not on the permanent-unlock list.

The gate

Because ascending is irreversible, it sits behind this project's destructive-action super-confirmation contract rather than a plain confirm dialog. design/prestige-gate.html specifies two independently operated key toggles, a confirmation slider that stays disabled until both keys are on, an animated but non-blocking progress fill, a distinct completion state, and an always-available emergency exit. It ships two variants: an ordinary prestige, and a full wipe rendered in the error role rather than the primary one.

The gate's own behaviour is deliberately not configurable. Softening a destructive-action confirmation defeats the point of having one. It is also not a security boundary — it is a safeguard against an accidental irreversible action, which is a different thing and should not be merged with this project's toy-lock language elsewhere.

Roadmap, not shipped

There is no ascend button and no gate in the application. The ascension curve, the reset semantics and the multiplier are what exists today.
